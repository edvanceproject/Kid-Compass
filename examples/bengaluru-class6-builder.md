## The ladder: Builder
**Explore** (home builds, VITM, makerspace) → **Show** (school science fair or the school round of the CBSE Science Exhibition) ← *she is here: building at home and ready for a first show-and-tell* → **Compete locally** (CBSE regional exhibition; NCSC district round) → **National** ([INSPIRE-MANAK](https://inspireawards-dst.gov.in), the school nominates her idea; NCSC national; the CBSE national exhibition; [School Innovation Marathon](https://aim.gov.in/) for teams) → **International** (through national selections, e.g. [WRO India](https://wroindia.org/season-2026/) to the WRO final, but that costs ₹20,000 per team and needs a coach, so it's not for now).

What moves her up a rung: write down **one problem she wants to fix** and build a model for it. A clear idea plus a "how I built it" page is exactly what INSPIRE-MANAK and the school fairs ask for. **Next rung this year:** a school-fair project, then an INSPIRE-MANAK idea in July 2027.

## Missed it? Plan for next time
- [INSPIRE Awards – MANAK](https://inspireawards-dst.gov.in): open to Classes 6–12, free, the school sends up to 5 ideas. It closed 15 Sep 2026. The next window is expected around **15 Jul – 15 Sep 2027** (*estimated*). Start an idea notebook now, and ask the teacher in June, because schools set an earlier internal cut-off.
- [National Children's Science Congress (NCSC)](https://n-csc.in/): a 2-student team with a guide teacher; she fits the junior group (ages 10–14 as of 31 Dec). Karnataka's 2026-27 deadline wasn't on the [KSCST page](https://kscst.org.in/n-csc_files/32_n-csc_2026_27/n-csc_2026.html) I could read. District rounds are usually in November, so this year has most likely closed (*check with the school*). The next round is expected around **Jul–Aug 2027** (*estimated*). Look for a classmate to team up with.
- [School Innovation Marathon (AIM, NITI Aayog)](https://aim.gov.in/): school teams of 3–5 solve a community problem. The last edition ran 23 Sep – 11 Oct 2025, and no 2026-27 round had been announced. The next window is uncertain, roughly **Sep–Oct 2027** (*estimated*).
- [VITM Bengaluru District Science Quiz](https://www.vismuseum.gov.in/): the 2026-27 preliminary test was on 23 Jul 2026. Next one expected around **July 2027** (*estimated*). Usually entered through the school.

## Balance check
In November, about 10–12 hours over the weekends: one museum trip, one makerspace visit, a Lift 2.0 build, and Bebras puzzles if the school signs up. It costs roughly **₹1,300–1,500**, including about ₹250–300 for each trip across town. That's right at your ₹1,500, so if a month runs over, skip the makerspace. December is lighter, at about ₹500–700 for the sky-watch and the festival. If she does get onto a CBSE exhibition team, drop the makerspace that month, because the exhibition build will take most of her weekends. Everything here is hands-on and fun first; only two items are competitions, and both are free.

### Free ways to prepare
- Lift 2.0 at home (above), written up with sketches. That's the start of a science-fair project.
- Try the past Bebras puzzles on [bebras.in](https://www.bebras.in) together, 15 minutes at a time.
- Keep an **idea notebook**: every time something at home is annoying or broken, she writes it down. Next July, pick the best one for INSPIRE-MANAK.
- Ask the school or a local library for books on simple machines (pulleys, levers, gears). Her lift already uses a pulley.
